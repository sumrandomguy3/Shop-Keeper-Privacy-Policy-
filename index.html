Shop Keeper — Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 28, 2026 · App: Shop Keeper (io.github.sumrandomguy3.shopkeeper)

The short version

Shop Keeper does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any data. Period. Everything you enter — jobs, clients, estimates, photos, expenses — is stored only in files on your own device. The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no user accounts, and no network communication of any kind.

Data storage

All app data is saved to the app's private storage on your device. It is never uploaded anywhere. If you delete the app, that data is deleted with it. You can create a backup file at any time (Settings → Export data); that file is yours, saved or shared only where you choose to put it.

Device permissions and why they're requested

  • Camera — only used when you choose to take a progress photo for a job. Photos are saved on your device inside the app's storage.
  • Photo library — only used when you choose to attach an existing photo to a job.
  • Calendar — only used when you tap Sync, to add job due dates and follow-up reminders to your phone's own calendar. Nothing is read from or sent to any external service.

Each permission is optional; the app works without them.

Sharing you initiate

When you share an estimate, invoice PDF, CSV export, or backup file, it is handed to the Android share sheet and goes only to the app or person you select. Shop Keeper has no visibility into, or copy of, anything you share. Tapping a client's contact chip opens your phone, messaging, or social app to reach them — Shop Keeper sends nothing itself.

Children

Shop Keeper is a business tool intended for adults and is not directed at children. Since no data is collected, no children's data is collected either.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app ever changes these practices, this page will be updated and the change noted in the app's release notes before it takes effect.
